Order of operations

  1. Brackets first: 2(3 + 1) = 2 × 4 = 8
  2. Then powers: 3 × 2² = 3 × 4 = 12
  3. Then multiplication and division, from left to right
  4. Then addition and subtraction, from left to right: 3 × 4 + 5 = 17

Combining like terms

Add the coefficients and keep the variable part: 5x − 2x + 3 + 4 = 3x + 7. Terms with different variable parts stay apart.

Solving a linear equation

  1. Combine like terms on each side.
  2. Move the variable terms to one side, doing the same to both sides.
  3. Undo additions and subtractions, then multiplications and divisions.
  4. Check: put the solution back into the original equation.
