Combining like terms

By the end you can spot the terms of an expression that belong together and write the expression in its shortest form.

1.Terms that belong together

Like terms have exactly the same variable part. 4x and 2x are like terms; so are 7 and −3, which are both constants. 4x and 4y are not, and neither are x and x².

2.Adding them up

To combine like terms, add their coefficients and keep the variable part. Think of 4x + 2x as four boxes of x plus two more boxes: six boxes, 6x.

In a longer expression, gather each kind separately. Keep the sign in front of each term with it:

  1. Start with 5x + 3 − 2x + 4
  2. Group the x terms and the constants: (5x − 2x) + (3 + 4)
  3. Combine each group: 3x + 7
Watch out: 3x + 7 cannot be shortened further. 3x and 7 are not like terms, so writing 10x would be wrong.

3.Check yourself

What is 6y − y + 2 in its shortest form?

y means 1y, so 6y − 1y = 5y. The constant 2 stays as it is.

After combining, 3n + 4 + 5n − 4 becomes ?n. What number goes in front of n?

3n + 5n = 8n, and the constants cancel: 4 − 4 = 0. The expression is 8n.
